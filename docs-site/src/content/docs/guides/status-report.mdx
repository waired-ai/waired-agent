**Status…** in the Waired app shows everything Waired knows right now, in one
window: this computer's engine, model, sharing, and routing, whether Claude
Code is pointed here, every other computer with what it is running, and any
recent notices. Every line in the menu that reports a state opens this window
when you select it. The **Status…** row is the one that says so.

The window shows the last reading, taken a few seconds earlier, and says at
the top when that was. Open it again for a fresh one.

## The sections

The first lines identify the computer and the state the menu's top line
reports:

```
Waired 0.0.3 (a1b2c3d)
my-desktop · 100.64.0.5 · my-network
Connected · read at 14:02:11
```

**THIS COMPUTER** repeats the status lines from the menu: the engine and its
state, the engine's own reason when it has one, the active model, the
sharing state, the routing setting in force, whether any of your other
computers' engines is reachable, and whether Claude Code is routed through
Waired.

**OTHER COMPUTERS — 2 of 4 serving** lists your other computers, one line
each, in the same form as the **This device** submenu: a glyph, the
computer's name, and the model it runs or the reason it cannot answer, plus
its hardware. The window shows the first few and says how many more are on
the clipboard.

**NOTICES** repeats the notice rows from the top of the menu. See
[Notices](/guides/notices/).

**RECENT** lists the requests in the last ten minutes that could not be
served the way you asked and were answered by something else. Empty is normal
and good. Each line reads `<model> — <from> → <to> (<reason>, <how long ago>)`.
The reason is one of `engine not ready`, `sharing off`, `at capacity`,
`legacy peer`, `auth error`, `transport error`, or `paused`.

## Copy details

**Copy details** puts a longer version of the report on your clipboard. It
is the version to paste into a bug report, because it carries what a support
thread asks for next:

- The host's operating system and the account.
- The engine's full reason, not the one-line form the menu fits.
- The state of the Claude Code, OpenCode, and OpenClaw integrations.
- Every other computer, with its identifier, its address on your private
  network, how packets reach it (`direct 12 ms` or `relay 48 ms`), and the
  last error its engine reported.
- A **MESH MAP** section: whether any other engine is reachable, when this
  computer last received the map of your network, how old that map is, and
  the limits it is judged against.

**Close** leaves your clipboard untouched.

<Aside type="note" title="Grey means unavailable">
	Lines that report a state are not greyed out, on purpose. On every
	operating system, grey on a menu means unavailable. So when you do see a
	greyed line, for example **Model not loaded**, it is telling you that
	particular thing cannot be done right now.
</Aside>
